# Customer Retention & Churn Analysis

## Data Science & Analytics – Task 2 (2026) | Future Interns

A data analytics project focused on understanding **customer churn, retention patterns, customer lifetime, and churn drivers** using the Telco Customer Churn dataset.

The goal of this project is to transform customer subscription data into **business-focused insights and actionable retention recommendations**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is a major challenge for subscription-based businesses. Understanding why customers leave and which customer segments are more likely to churn can help businesses improve retention and reduce customer loss.

In this project, customer subscription data is analyzed to:

* Measure overall customer churn and retention
* Identify churn patterns across different customer segments
* Analyze churn based on contract type and tenure
* Study customer lifetime patterns
* Perform cohort-style analysis using customer tenure groups
* Identify important retention and churn drivers
* Provide practical recommendations to reduce churn

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

1. Clean and organize the customer dataset.

3. Analyze churn patterns across customer segments.

6. Identify factors associated with customer churn.

## 📈 Analysis Performed

### 1. Churn & Retention Analysis

The overall churn rate and retention rate were calculated to understand the current customer retention situation.

### 2. Contract Analysis

Customer churn was analyzed across different contract types to identify contracts associated with higher churn.

### 3. Tenure Analysis

Customers were grouped according to their tenure to understand how customer lifetime relates to churn.

### 4. Customer Lifetime Analysis

Average customer tenure was compared between churned and retained customers.

### 5. Cohort Analysis

A tenure-based cohort analysis was performed because the Telco dataset does not contain actual customer signup dates.

### 6. Internet Service Analysis

Churn rates were compared across different internet service categories.

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis highlights the following business patterns:

* Customer churn is an important customer retention challenge.
* Customers with shorter tenure are generally more likely to churn.
* Contract type has a noticeable relationship with customer churn.
* Longer-tenure customers generally demonstrate better retention.
* Different internet service categories show different churn behavior.

---

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions can help improve customer retention:

### 1. Focus on New Customers

Create early-stage retention campaigns for customers with shorter tenure.

### 2. Encourage Long-Term Contracts

Offer incentives, discounts, or additional benefits to encourage customers to move toward longer-term contracts.

### 3. Target High-Risk Customers

Use customer behavior and subscription characteristics to identify customers who may be at higher risk of churn.

### 4. Improve Customer Experience

Strengthen customer support and service quality to address potential sources of dissatisfaction.

### 5. Monitor Churn Regularly

Track churn across customer segments to identify emerging retention problems early.
